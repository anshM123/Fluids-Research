### R006 — P02 CCF self-similar profiles: classical solver reproduces the AI-discovered unstable singularity
- **Hypothesis:** smooth CCF profiles are members of a continuous λ-family singled out by the local exponent p(λ) = λ/(1+λ+h1) ∈ 2ℕ at the origin.
- **Computation:** log-variable formulation η = ln ξ, Θ = e^{cη}Ψ; exact Hilbert-transform Fourier multiplier −i tanh(π(k−ic)/2) (validated to 1e−12); integral (shooting) form of the profile ODE to avoid the index-1 spurious constraint of square spectral discretisations; 8th-order cumulative quadrature; Picard + Newton. N=2048, η∈[−30,150], c=0.75.
- **Result:** p=2 profile at **λ = 0.6056991270** (22 s CPU), reproducing the first unstable CCF singularity λ₁≈0.6057 of Eggers–Fontelos 2020 / Wang et al. 2025. The family exists for λ∈[0.48,0.95] (fixed-λ Newton); p(λ) non-monotone: max 2.197 at λ≈0.83, min ≈1.91 near λ≈0.50, rising again as λ→0.47 (published λ₂=0.4703).
- **Interpretation:** the ladder may be generated by *oscillations of p(λ) about 2* along one branch (not by p=4,6,…). Needs arclength continuation through folds.
- **Cost:** ≈0.5 CPU-h · **Status:** ACTIVE → DISCOVERY candidate

### R007 — P02: all known CCF self-similar profiles lie on ONE connected solution branch
- **Hypothesis:** smooth profiles = crossings p(λ)=2 of a single continuous family; ladder generated by oscillations of p along the branch.
- **Computation:** matrix-free Newton–Krylov (FFT Hilbert multiplier, 8th-order cumulative quadrature, GMRES 2–30 its), pseudo-arclength continuation with far-field-amplitude normalisation (the earlier Ψ(0)=const normalisation intersects the scaling-symmetry orbit twice → spurious folds; fixed). On-the-fly crossing detection + bordered Newton refinement (λ unknown, p=2). N = 16k … 262k points on η∈[−30,120], c=0.7.
- **Result (N=16k–64k):**
  - λ₀ = 1.1807776629 (β=λ/(1+λ)=0.54144798) — literature stable-profile exponent γ=0.5414465 ("accurate to six digits"): **match** → stable profile.
  - λ₁ = 0.6057337012 (N=16k and 32k agree to 10 digits) — literature 0.6057: **match**; its unstable eigenvalue from our linear-stability operator μ≈0.366 vs literature μ=0.36525: **match**; trivial time-translation eigenvalue μ=1 reproduced exactly.
  - λ₂ ≈ 0.4713 (N=64k), 0.4719 (N=16k) — literature 0.4703; this profile has an internal near-sonic layer (1+λ+HΘ/ξ ≈ 0.03, width ≈ 0.012 in η) → resolution-limited; N≥128k runs in progress.
  - Candidate λ₃ ≈ 0.57 (N=16k: 0.5747/0.5661, under-resolved; layer width ≈ 0.003).
  - Beyond λ₃ the branch approaches a sonic limit (min(1+λ+G) → 0) while λ oscillates over [0.38, 1.95].
- **Interpretation:** stable + 1st + 2nd unstable profiles are p=2 points of one connected family; the ladder is non-monotone in λ (λ₃ > λ₂) — explains why searches below λ₂ (arXiv:2511.22819) found nothing. Increasingly thin internal sonic layers explain the difficulty of PINN approaches.
- **Cost:** ≈4 CPU-h · **Status:** DISCOVERY (λ₃ pending high-resolution confirmation)
