# Suggested and excluded referees (optional in the submission system)

The editors choose referees. Suggestions help most when they cover the expertise the paper needs and avoid
conflicts. Do not suggest recent collaborators, people at your own institutions, or anyone with a personal or
financial interest in the outcome.

## Expertise the paper needs

1. **Self-similar blow-up of incompressible flow and computer-assisted proofs.** Boussinesq and axisymmetric Euler
   with a boundary, IPM, model equations (refs 1–3, 7, 9, 10, 18–22 of the manuscript).
2. **Neural and high-precision computation of unstable self-similar profiles**, the methods whose depth limit the
   paper quantifies (refs 4–6, 8).
3. **Exponential asymptotics and WKB resonance conditions**: transcendentally small terms and Stokes phenomena
   (refs 13–17).
4. **Numerical analysis of continuation and Newton–Krylov methods**: discretization error, round-off and grid
   effects, spectral counts (refs 11, 12, 26–33).
5. **Reproducibility and pre-registration in computational science** (refs 23–25).

The authors of the cited work in areas 1–3 are the natural experts. The paper starts from the neural computations
of the same hierarchies in refs 6 and 8. Consider whether their authors should be suggested, or listed as excluded
because of competing work.
